PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
₹6,000 / year

Who is it for: Small & marginal farmer families with land

Farmer families with cultivable land receive ₹6,000 a year in three instalments, straight into their bank account.

हिन्दी में: पीएम-किसान सम्मान निधि — कृषि योग्य ज़मीन रखने वाले किसान परिवारों को हर साल ₹6,000 की तीन किस्तों में सीधे बैंक खाते में मिलते हैं।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Land ownership records (khatauni / 7-12 extract)
  3. Bank passbook (linked to Aadhaar)
  4. Self-register at pmkisan.gov.in or visit your nearest CSC
