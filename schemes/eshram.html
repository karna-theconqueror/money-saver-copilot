e-Shram Card (Unorganised Workers)

Ministry of Labour & Employment
₹2 L accident cover + pension link

Who is it for: Unorganised workers aged 16–59 (not in EPFO/ESIC)

One registration unlocks accident insurance and puts you on the list for future social-security schemes for labourers, domestic workers, street workers and farmers.

हिन्दी में: ई-श्रम कार्ड — असंगठित कामगारों के लिए एक रजिस्ट्रेशन — दुर्घटना बीमा और भविष्य की सामाजिक सुरक्षा योजनाओं का लाभ।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card (mobile-linked for OTP)
  2. Bank passbook
  3. Register free at eshram.gov.in or any CSC
