PM Awas Yojana (Gramin)

Ministry of Rural Development
~₹1.20 L housing assistance

Who is it for: Rural households without a pucca house (BPL / SECC listed)

Around ₹1.20 lakh of assistance to build a pucca house in rural areas, plus 90–95 days of free unskilled labour under MGNREGS.

हिन्दी में: पीएम आवास योजना (ग्रामीण) — गाँव में पक्का मकान न होने पर मकान बनाने के लिए सरकारी सहायता और मुफ़्त श्रम।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Job card / SECC listing proof
  3. Bank passbook
  4. Apply at pmayg.nic.in or Gram Panchayat / block office
