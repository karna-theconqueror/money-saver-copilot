Subsidised Food Grain (NFSA / Ration)

Department of Food & Public Distribution
~₹15,000 / year in food grain value

Who is it for: Eligible low-income households (₹35 kg rice/wheat at ~₹3/kg)

A family of four gets 140 kg of rice or wheat a month at about ₹3/kg — worth roughly ₹15,000 a year at market prices.

हिन्दी में: रियायती अनाज (राशन योजना) — पात्र परिवारों को लगभग ₹3 प्रति किलो की दर से अनाज — बाज़ार मूल्य पर यह हर साल लगभग ₹15,000 की बचत है।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Address proof
  3. Family details / marriage certificate if needed
  4. Apply at nfsa.gov.in or your local ration office
