Indira Gandhi Old Age Pension (NSAP)

Ministry of Rural Development
₹300 – ₹500 / month

Who is it for: Senior citizens aged 60+ from BPL households

A monthly central pension for older people with little or no income — many eligible families never apply.

हिन्दी में: इंदिरा गांधी वृद्धावस्था पेंशन — 60 साल से ऊपर, कम आय वाले बुज़ुर्गों को हर महीने सरकारी पेंशन — बहुत पात्र परिवार आवेदन ही नहीं करते।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Age proof
  3. BPL card / income declaration
  4. Apply at your Gram Panchayat, block office, or via CSC
