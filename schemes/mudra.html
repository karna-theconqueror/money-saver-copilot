MUDRA Loan (PMMY)

SIDBI / public sector banks
Up to ₹10 L collateral-free business loan

Who is it for: Self-employed, shopkeepers, traders, food vendors, artisans

Shishu (up to ₹50k), Kishore (up to ₹5L) and Tarun (up to ₹10L) tranches — no collateral, government-guaranteed.

हिन्दी में: मुद्रा ऋण (पीएमएमवाई) — दुकान, ठेला, व्यापार या छोटे काम के लिए बिना गारंटी ₹50,000 से ₹10 लाख तक का ऋण।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Business plan (one page is enough)
  3. Bank passbook
  4. Apply at any bank branch or pmmudra.org.in
