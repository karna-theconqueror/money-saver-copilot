PM Awas Yojana (Urban)

Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs
Up to ₹2.35 L one-time subsidy

Who is it for: Families without a pucca house, income up to ₹8 lakh

A capital subsidy plus a cheaper home loan if you don’t own a pucca house in an urban area — the single biggest housing benefit in India.

हिन्दी में: पीएम आवास योजना (शहरी) — शहर में पक्का मकान न होने पर सरकारी सब्सिडी और सस्ता होम लोन — सबसे बड़ा आवास लाभ।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Income certificate
  3. Bank / loan papers
  4. Apply at pmay-urban.gov.in or via CSC
