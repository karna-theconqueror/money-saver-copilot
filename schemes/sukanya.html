Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

Ministry of Finance / India Post
8.2% tax-free interest

Who is it for: Parents of a girl child under 10 years

The highest guaranteed-return savings scheme in India — interest is fully tax-free and the money is locked for her education and marriage.

हिन्दी में: सुकन्या समृद्धि योजना — 10 साल से छोटी बेटी के नाम पर बचत — देश की सबसे ऊँची गारंटीड ब्याज दर, पूरी तरह कर-मुक्त।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Girl child’s birth certificate
  2. Guardian’s Aadhaar
  3. Open at any post office or authorised bank
  4. Invest at least ₹250 / year
