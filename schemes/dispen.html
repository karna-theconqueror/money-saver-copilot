Disability Pension (NSAP — IGNDPS)

Ministry of Rural Development
₹300 – ₹500 / month

Who is it for: Persons with severe disability (80%+) aged 18–59 in BPL households

A monthly pension for people with 80% or more disability who cannot work — covers both rural and urban areas.

हिन्दी में: दिव्यांग पेंशन (आईजीएनडीपीएस) — गंभीर दिव्यांगता (80%+) वाले 18–59 आयु के लोगों को मासिक पेंशन।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Disability certificate (UDID card)
  3. BPL card / income declaration
  4. Apply at the block / municipal office or via CSC
