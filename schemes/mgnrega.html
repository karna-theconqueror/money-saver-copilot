MGNREGS — 100 Days Guaranteed Work

Ministry of Rural Development
~₹22,000 / year (100 days at local wage)

Who is it for: Rural households willing to do unskilled manual work

A legal guarantee of 100 days of wage work per rural household per year, with wages paid directly to your bank account.

हिन्दी में: मनरेगा — 100 दिन की गारंटीशुदा रोज़गार — ग्रामीण परिवारों को हर साल 100 दिनों का कानूनी अधिकार — मज़दूरी सीधे बैंक खाते में।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Bank passbook
  3. Register for a Job Card at Gram Panchayat / block office
  4. Demand work in writing and keep the receipt
