PM SVANidhi (Street Vendor Loan)

Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs
₹50,000 working-capital loan + interest subsidy

Who is it for: Street vendors and small shop owners in urban areas

A collateral-free working loan with an interest subsidy, and a cashback reward for repaying on time — built for the smallest businesses.

हिन्दी में: पीएम स्वनिधि — रेहड़ी-पटरी ऋण — छोटे दुकानदारों को बिना गारंटी कार्यशील पूँजी ऋण, ब्याज सब्सिडी और समय पर चुकाने पर कैशबैक।

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Certificate of vending / letter from ULB (or a vendor declaration)
  3. Bank passbook
  4. Apply via pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in or a CSC / bank
